The fox face is one of the first models many children fold: it needs only six steps and a pen. It also makes a sweet bookmark, a finger puppet or a card. Fold a few in orange, red and white and you have a whole family.

{props.steps}

## Ideas

- **Make a family.** Small squares make cubs; bigger ones make a parent.
- **Add a tail.** Fold a long, thin triangle from a second square and stick it behind the face.
- **Meet a sleepy fox.** Our [Fox asleep on the moon](/products/fox-asleep-on-the-moon) papercraft kit folds a faceted fox curled up in a crescent moon. It is a longer build for older children and grown-ups.
