Origami is the art of folding paper into shapes without cutting or gluing. All it takes is a square of paper, a flat table and a little patience. This guide covers the paper, the words and the main fold symbols you will meet in the other guides on this site.

## Which paper to use

- **Origami paper (kami)** is thin, crisp and usually coloured on one side and white on the other. A 15 cm (6 in) square is the easiest size for small hands.
- **Double-sided paper** is coloured on both sides, so the inside of a fold shows a second colour. Our [double-sided origami paper](/products/double-sided-origami-paper) is good for cranes, hearts and frogs.
- **Patterned paper** turns a plain model into a gift. Try our [patterned origami paper](/products/patterned-origami-paper) for boats and cranes.
- **Glitter and foil papers** hold a crease very well but do not like being unfolded many times, so save them for models you know. Our [iridescent glitter paper](/products/iridescent-glitter-paper) is thin enough for a crane.
- **Printer paper** works for practice. It is thicker, so press each crease firmly.

## Words you will see

- **Edge** is a straight side of the paper, **corner** is where two edges meet, and **point** is any corner of the folded model.
- **Crease** is the line a fold leaves behind.
- **Flap** is any part of the model you can lift and fold on its own.
- **Base** is a starting shape that many models share, such as the square base used for the crane.
- **Repeat behind** means do the same fold on the back of the model.

## Tips for clean folds

- Fold on a hard, flat table, not on your lap.
- Line up the corners and edges exactly before you crease, then press from the middle out to the ends.
- If a step looks confusing, look ahead at the next drawing to see the shape you are aiming for.
- Small hands: start with bigger paper. Grown-ups can pre-crease the tricky folds.

## What to fold next

Try the [origami heart](/guides/heart) or the [paper boat](/guides/boat) first; they use simple valley folds. Then move on to the [fox face](/guides/fox), the [jumping frog](/guides/frog), and the [paper crane](/guides/crane) when you are ready for a reverse fold.
