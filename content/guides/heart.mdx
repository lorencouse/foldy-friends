The origami heart is quick, uses only valley folds, and makes a lovely gift: tuck one into a card, a lunch box or a book. Paper coloured on one side gives a heart that is coloured all over, and patterned paper makes it extra special.

{props.steps}

## Ideas for your hearts

- **A garland.** Fold a dozen and thread them on a string.
- **A gift tag.** Punch a small hole near the top and thread a ribbon through.
- **A message.** Write a note on the white side before you fold, and it hides inside.
