This simple panda face lets the paper do the work: one side makes the face, the other the ears. You need paper that is black or another dark colour on one side and white on the other, and a black marker for the eye patches.

There is no single traditional panda face in origami. This is the simplest common one, easy enough for young folders.

{props.steps}

## Tips

- **The higher the fold in step 5, the smaller the ears.** Try a few and pick your favourite.
- **No black paper?** Colour one side of a sheet of printer paper with a black crayon, then cut it square.
- **Draw the patches big.** Wide, droopy eye patches make it look most like a panda.
