This friendly elephant face has big ears and a trunk folded like a fan. It is a lovely model for practising pleats, and it looks great on a card or a bookmark. It only uses valley folds, apart from the trunk, where valley and mountain folds take turns.

{props.steps}

## Tips

- **Line the edges up with the centre crease** in step 3, and the ears will match.
- **Small pleats, lots of them.** On a 15 cm square, pleats about the width of a pencil look best.
- **Big paper, big ears.** A 20 or 25 cm square is easier for small hands.
