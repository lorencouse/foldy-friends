The paper boat is a model nearly everyone learns from a grandparent: first a hat, then a boat. Printer paper is the right size and shape, and newspaper makes a big one. It floats until the paper soaks through, so have a few ready for bath time.

{props.steps}

## Tips

- **Crease hard.** A boat with sharp creases holds its shape on the water.
- **Steps 6 and 8 are the tricky ones.** Go slowly, and let the paper flatten along the folds it already has.
- **Make it last.** Rub a wax crayon over the outside of the hull and it floats for longer.
- **Decorate first.** Draw portholes, a name or a flag on the paper before you fold.
