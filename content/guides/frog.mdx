This is the traditional jumping frog, folded by children all over the world. Instead of a square it starts from a rectangle twice as tall as it is wide, so cut a square of origami paper in half, or use an index card. The zigzag at the back is a spring: press it and let go, and the frog leaps.

{props.steps}

## Make it jump further

- **Use stiffer paper.** An index card or thick origami paper springs best; thin paper flops.
- **Do not flatten the spring.** The zigzag needs a little air in it to push.
- **Press and slide.** Press the very back of the frog down, then slide your finger off backwards.
- **Have a race.** Fold a frog each and see whose jumps furthest on a smooth table.
